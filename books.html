The Book of Everlasting Destiny

Frank Reyes. Coming soon to Amazon in paperback and ebook.

Destiny is the hallway. Free will is the walk.

The Book of Everlasting Destiny began as letters Frank Reyes wrote to the people he loves: after ceremonies, after conversations that split a heart open, at three in the morning when a question would not sit down. Distilled into thirteen chapters, those letters become a grounded map for anyone who has felt the pull of something larger and still has to show up on Monday.

Vekovechnaya sud'ba, Russian for everlasting destiny, is not a script you cannot refuse. It is a theme your higher self chose and a walk only you can take. Inside you will find:

  • How a single life hangs from Source, oversoul, and higher mind, and why trying to escape the density never works
  • Angels and guides as offices of clean energy, not mascots
  • The loops that repeat until you see them, and how to drop a different track
  • Shadow as unused light, and the crowd of many I's that pretends to be one driver
  • Stillness, prayer, breath, and meditation as a daily practice instead of a weekend costume

This is not a spiritual brochure. It is an honest, illustrated companion for carrying light through an ordinary afternoon without advertising it.

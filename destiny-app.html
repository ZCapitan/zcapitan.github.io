About the app

A daily companion to The Book of Everlasting Destiny by Frank Reyes. Each day brings one small step from the book, a moment of stillness and a gentle angel message. A private journal helps you reflect on what comes up in meditation and in plant medicine integration, guided by the book's themes.

Features

  • Destiny Path One daily step drawn from the book.
  • Insight Journal Guided reflection prompts for your meditations and your integration after plant medicine journeys. It is for reflection only and gives no medical advice.
  • Letters to Your Future Self Write letters you'll open later along the way.
  • Daily Angel and Stillness Short daily moments that tie into the ZCapitan Shorts.
  • Private by design Your journal stays yours. Adults 18 and over only.

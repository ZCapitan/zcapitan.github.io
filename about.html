About Frank

Frank Reyes, spiritual teacher and author of The Book of Everlasting Destiny.

Welcome. Everlasting Destiny is a calm, grounded place to begin your spiritual journey. On the ZCapitan YouTube channel you will find guided meditations, plus Shorts on the chakras, starseeds and the angelic orders, shared simply and with an open heart.

My story

I was not always open to any of this. My parents came from Peru, and I was born and raised in America. I became a Division I swimmer and water polo player through determination, not natural talent. Basketball was the sport I really loved. Crystals, tarot cards, mystical subjects? I was the guy making fun of them. So yes, I understand the skepticism.

Over time, experiences and conversations made me question what I thought I knew. I started writing to people I loved, then trying to make sense of things on the page. Those letters became The Book of Everlasting Destiny. Before Destiny is where I am working through the personal story behind it. I am part of that story, not someone standing outside it with all the answers.

What I teach

I teach meditation, prayer and self observation. I talk about chakras, starseeds and angelic orders as part of my spiritual outlook, not as scientific facts. But I keep coming back to ordinary life. What am I repeating? Why do I need to win this conversation? Can I notice an impulse without letting it decide what I do?

In The Book of Everlasting Destiny, I explore destiny alongside free will. I believe a life can have a direction without every choice being decided in advance. To me, spiritual practice is not an escape from being human. It is paying attention to how I speak, how I treat people and whether I do what I said I would. I have to practice that too.
